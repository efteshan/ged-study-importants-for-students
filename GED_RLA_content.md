## Question 1: Absolute Words Trap 1
### 💡 Core RLA Concept
Absolute Words: Look for answer choices containing words like always, never, everyone, nobody, all, none, must, only.
### 📜 Context & Explanation
* **Strategy Reminder:** GED reading passages rarely provide enough evidence to prove something happens 100% of the time. Correct answers are often more moderate (e.g., some, many, often).
### 🧠 GED Practice Problems
#### Practice 1
**Passage:** [Excerpt from a Human Resources Memo]
"In recent months, the management team has reviewed the effectiveness of the new telecommuting policy. While a vocal group of employees has reported challenges with the remote communication tools, productivity metrics indicate that a significant portion of the staff has maintained or even exceeded their previous output levels. The company will continue to offer flexible scheduling to most departments, recognizing that many workers benefit from a hybrid approach."
**Question:** Which statement is best supported by the memo?
* **A)** All employees have struggled with remote communication tools.
* **B)** Many workers have maintained their productivity while telecommuting.
* **C)** Nobody wants to return to the office full-time.
* **D)** The company will always offer flexible scheduling in the future.
* **Answer:** B (Choices A, C, and D contain absolute words 'All', 'Nobody', and 'always'. The text states a 'significant portion' and 'many workers' benefit, making B the best moderate choice.)
