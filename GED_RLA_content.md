## Question 1: Absolute Words Trap 1
### 💡 Core RLA Concept
Absolute Words: Look for answer choices containing words like always, never, everyone, nobody, all, none, must, only.
### 📜 Context & Explanation
* **Strategy Reminder:** GED reading passages rarely provide enough evidence to prove something happens all the time. Correct answers are often more moderate (e.g., some, many, often).
### 🧠 GED Practice Problems
#### Practice 1
**Passage:** Many citizens voted for the new tax bill to improve schools.
**Question:** Which statement is best supported by the passage?
* **A)** All citizens voted for the bill.
* **B)** Many citizens supported the tax bill.
* **C)** Nobody opposed the new tax bill.
* **D)** The tax bill will always improve schools.
* **Answer:** B (Choices A, C, and D contain absolute words 'All', 'Nobody', and 'always'. Choice B accurately uses 'Many'.)
